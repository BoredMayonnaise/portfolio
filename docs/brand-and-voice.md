# Mark Ocampos — brand and voice

## Positioning
An independent web engineer serving clients directly, with thoughtful implementation, care for privacy, and an eye for design.

**Homepage:** Mark Ocampos — Independent web engineer.

**Introduction:** I build web applications and thoughtful interfaces, turning complex ideas into clear digital experiences.

## Voice
Use clear, confident first-person language. Describe the actual work before naming the technology. Use “I” and “my,” reserving “we” for collaboration with a client.

Primary navigation uses Projects, About, Journal, and Contact. Arcade labels such as Levels, Player 1, Log, and Player 2 are supporting language. Calls to action explain their destination: View projects, Contact, and Send message.

Avoid invented metrics, clients, testimonials, availability, implied certifications, skill percentages, and absolute security promises. Project descriptions, roles, and outcomes come from real project records; omit missing details.

## Visual identity
- Cyber black `#090B03`: canvas.
- Pixel white `#DEEFEF`: typography.
- Hyper acid `#00FF66`: emphasis and primary actions, with dark text on filled buttons.
- Space Mono: game headings, navigation, and short metadata. Sora: descriptions, case studies, and forms. Fonts remain locally hosted.
- Keep the personal monogram, pixel borders, collectible hands, profile card, and short pixel transitions.
- Grid, glow, and scanlines belong behind content. Do not cover text, form controls, or product screenshots with effects.
- Preserve project-image compositions instead of cropping previews.

## Content and interaction
The title screen keeps Start, adds a clear personal introduction, and provides ordinary links to Projects and Contact. Visitors can reach work without playing or enabling JavaScript.

Game scenes synchronize with URL hashes and browser history. Scene changes manage focus, keyboard controls preserve native actions, and reduced-motion visitors bypass transitions. Short screens and increased text sizes scroll naturally.

Projects and case studies remain managed through Django admin. Capabilities link to real examples instead of numerical self-ratings.

Contact starts with name, email, and message. Keep the guided eight-question brief as an optional expandable section. Explain next steps without promising a response time. Preserve CSRF, validation, honeypot, database-backed limits, and saved messages when email notifications fail.

The canonical public origin is https://markocampos.rhymvex.space. Keep canonical, social, sitemap, and robots URLs aligned with it.
