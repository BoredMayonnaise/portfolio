01

what I do

six things, no filler

data protection

Encryption in motion and at rest, the least data collected, and safe defaults. I design so that leaking is the hard thing, not the default.

cryptography

Hashes, key exchange, one-way functions. I genuinely love the maths that keeps a secret — and the discipline of proving it stays that way.

logic & systems

Correct by construction, auditable, boring in the best way. Clean Python, JavaScript, and Django backends with behaviour you can reason about.

modern frontends

Fluid, reactive, and snappy interfaces. I pair Vite and Vue to build user experiences that feel instantly responsive without bloating the bundle.

performance

Every byte matters. I optimize payloads, caching layers, and database queries to ensure the applications feel incredibly fast in every condition.

photography

A second pair of eyes. Composition, light, restraint — the same discipline that keeps my code minimal finds its way into my frames.
